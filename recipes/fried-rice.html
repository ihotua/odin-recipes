Nigerian Fried Rice

Description

Nigerian fried rice is a colorful and flavorful rice dish made with cooked rice, mixed vegetables, spices, and stock. It is a popular dish at parties, celebrations, and family gatherings across Nigeria.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of rice
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • Green peas
  • Sweet corn
  • Green beans
  • Red and green bell peppers
  • Onions
  • Vegetable oil
  • Chicken stock
  • Curry powder and thhyme
  • Salt and seasoning cubes

Steps

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly and cook until almost tender.
  2. Wash and chop the carrots, green beans, bell peppers, and onions.
  3. Heat vegetable oil in a large frying pan.
  4. Add the onions and vegetables, then stir-fry for a few minutes.
  5. Add curry powder, thyme, salt, and seasoning cubes.
  6. Add the partially cooked rice and some chicken stock.
  7. Stir everything together and cook until the rice is tender.
  8. Serve hot with chicken, beef, or your preferred protein.
