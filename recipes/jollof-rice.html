Jollof Rice

Description

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions, and spices. It is enjoyed at family gatherings, parties, and celebrations.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of rice
  • Fresh tomatoes
  • Red bell peppers
  • Onions
  • Tomato paste
  • Vegetable oil
  • Stock and seasoning
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly and set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, and onions.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil in a pot and fry the tomato paste.
  4. Add the blended mixture, stock, and seasoning. Cook the sauce.
  5. Add the rice and wnough liquid to cook it.
  6. Cover the pot and cook until the rice is tender.
  7. Stir gently and serve hot.
